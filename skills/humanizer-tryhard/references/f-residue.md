# F. Residue and format

1. Inspect the actual deliverable for assistant correspondence, process assurances, template residue, tool tokens, and unfinished text.
2. Use the destination's formatting conventions. Check links, headings, reference syntax, and placeholders.
3. Preserve required disclosures, safety qualifications, and intentionally requested templates.
4. Recheck meaning and necessary limitations before G.

## Examples and boundaries

**Defective article text:** “Certainly! Here is a neutral, well-sourced overview. Let me know if you want more detail.”
**Repair:** Remove the assistant's introduction and offer; deliver the article.
**Preserve:** A customer-service email may legitimately invite a reply. Judge who is speaking to whom and whether the line serves the actual brief.

**Given:** The supplied report does not name the founder.
**Defective:** “The founder keeps a low profile, and biographical information is not publicly available.”
**Repair:** Omit the unsupported biography. If the missing fact affects the task, say separately that the supplied report does not identify the founder.
**Why:** A gap in available material proves neither privacy preferences nor the absence of public information.
**Preserve:** A substantive limitation such as “The study followed patients for six weeks” constrains interpretation and belongs in the piece.

**Defective final copy:** “Founded in [YEAR], the company...” with `INSERT_SOURCE_URL` or an internal citation token.
**Repair:** Fill from verified material, remove the unsupported clause, or identify the unresolved requirement. Never guess the year or source.
**Preserve:** A requested reusable template should retain its intentional blanks. Code examples can legitimately contain placeholder domains.

Check date placeholders, unclosed markup, undefined or unused reference names, accidental code fences, and abrupt cutoffs. Markdown is appropriate in Markdown destinations; Wikipedia syntax belongs on Wikipedia. Routine statements that information was “preserved” or policy followed do not belong inside the deliverable. Wikipedia-specific submission statements, maintenance templates, canned user pages, permission requests, and edit summaries matter only when that is the actual destination. Do not strip required AI disclosures or replace necessary limitations with canned assurances.
