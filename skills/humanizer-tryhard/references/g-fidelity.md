# G. Fidelity and usefulness

1. Compare the revised copy with the factual ledger and the user's brief, not merely the previous pass.
2. Check names, numbers, dates, technical terms, exact quotations, attribution, uncertainty, and commitments.
3. Check what disappeared and what was added. Restore useful specifics and purposeful voice; remove quiet inventions.
4. Resolve findings or record the specific open constraint. Then proceed to the next full round or final hostile read as SKILL.md directs.

## Examples and boundaries

**Given:** Forty testers received a beta on Tuesday. Some reported slow exports. No fix was promised.
**Defective revision:** “We shipped Tuesday to 40 testers and are improving export speed based on their feedback.”
**Repair:** “We shipped the beta on Tuesday to 40 testers. Some reported slow exports.”
**Why:** The rewrite invented a commitment. Plausibility is insufficient.
**Preserve:** If the supplied material actually commits to a fix, retain that commitment and its stated deadline or uncertainty.

**Given quotation:** “The pilot may reduce delays.”
**Defective revision:** “The pilot will reduce delays.”
**Repair:** Restore the exact quotation. A change of certainty changes the claim.
**Preserve:** A user-authorized paraphrase can change wording, but must still preserve “may” and attribution.

**Given:** A repair requires disconnecting power before opening the case.
**Defective shortening:** “Open the case and replace the fuse.”
**Repair:** Restore the power-disconnection instruction.
**Why:** Brevity cannot remove a condition needed to use the advice safely.

For the final hostile read, compare generic sentences with the reader's purpose. Keep necessary context and supplied human feeling; also keep purposeful humor, emphasis, or rhythm when it contributes to the author's intent. An accurate but empty sentence may still deserve deletion. A clean passage may need no edits at all.

Caveats apply to every pass: stylistic signs are fallible and change over time. This workflow edits prose; it cannot certify authorship or guarantee detector outcomes. Wikipedia's detection examples are evidence to study, not a universal style specification. Do not infer authorship from isolated vocabulary, punctuation, grammar, missing citations, or blandness. Source dates, editor histories, and detector research are maintenance context, not checks to run on every writing task.
